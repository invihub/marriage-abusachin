Travel Plans & Hotels

🚂 Pearl City Superfast Express

  • Train 12693: Chennai Egmore (20:00) ➔ Tuticorin (07:15).
  • Train 12694: Tuticorin (20:20) ➔ Chennai Egmore (07:35).
  • Overnight AC travel connecting Chennai & Thoothukudi.

✈️ Direct Flights (MAA ⇄ TCR)

  • Daily IndiGo nonstop flight (~1 hr 25 mins).
  • Tuticorin Airport (TCR) is ~15 km from town venues.
  • Chennai Airport (MAA) is ~14 km from The Leela Palace.

🚌 AC Sleeper Buses

  • Overnight Volvo / Scania sleepers (IntrCity, KPN, SRM).
  • Boarding: Koyambedu CMBT / Guindy / Perungalathur.
  • Drop: Tuticorin New Bus Stand (~9 hrs).
