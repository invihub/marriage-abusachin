Travel Plans & Hotel Stays

Everything you need to travel seamlessly between Chennai and Thoothukudi, along with curated accommodations near all venues.

🚂

Pearl City Superfast Express

Train No: 12693 (MS ➔ TN) / 12694 (TN ➔ MS)
  • From Chennai Egmore (MS): Departs 20:00, Arrives Tuticorin (TN) 07:15 (+1 day).
  • From Tuticorin (TN): Departs 20:20, Arrives Chennai Egmore 07:35 (+1 day).
  • Coaches: 1st AC (1A), 2nd AC (2A), 3rd AC (3A), Sleeper (SL).
  • Booking Tip: Connects Chennai and Thoothukudi overnight in absolute comfort. Book on IRCTC.co.in.
✈️

Direct Flights (MAA ⇄ TCR)

Chennai International ⇄ Tuticorin Airport
  • Flight Time: Approx. 1 hour 25 mins nonstop daily via IndiGo.
  • Tuticorin Airport (TCR): Located at Vagaikulam, ~15 km from Tuticorin town & wedding venues.
  • Chennai Airport (MAA): ~14 km from The Leela Palace, MRC Nagar.
  • Cabs: Pre-paid taxis and Uber/Ola available at both airports.
🚌

Luxury AC Sleeper Buses

Overnight Multi-Axle Volvo / Scania
  • Key Operators: IntrCity SmartBus, KPN, SRM Travels, Parveen Travels.
  • Boarding in Chennai: Koyambedu CMBT, Guindy, Perungalathur, OMR.
  • Drop in Tuticorin: New Bus Stand, Tuticorin Bypass, GRT Junction.
  • Duration: ~9 to 10 hours via smooth NH45 / NH38 highway.

Iconic Spots: Thoothukudi & Chennai

Handpicked must-visit landmarks that capture the heart and soul of Abu's and Sachin's hometowns.

Thoothukudi

Tiruchendur Murugan Temple

Ancient 2,000-year-old coastal sanctuary where the soaring stone Gopuram overlooks crashing Bay of Bengal waves. One of the sacred Arupadaiveedu shrines.

Thoothukudi

Pearl City Beach & Harbour

Breezy shoreline known for India's historic natural pearl diving legacy, shimmering salt pans, and sunset pier walks with cool ocean winds.

Thoothukudi

Ganesh Bakery Cashew Macaroons

Tuticorin's iconic confection! Golden conical meringues crafted with egg whites and roasted cashews that melt on your tongue. Don't leave without a box!

Chennai

Kapaleeshwarar Temple & Filter Coffee

7th-century Dravidian architectural marvel in Mylapore. Follow morning darshan with a steaming brass davara tumbler of authentic degree filter coffee.

Chennai

Marina Beach & Napier Bridge

The world's second-longest natural urban beach, paired with the iconic illuminated Napier Bridge over the Cooum estuary, famous for evening sundal and sea breeze.

Chennai

Mahabalipuram Shore Temples

UNESCO World Heritage site along the East Coast Road (ECR). 7th-century rock-cut monolithic rathas, Arjuna's Penance, and shore temples bathed in golden sunlight.
